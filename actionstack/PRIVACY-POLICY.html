Privacy Policy ​

Effective date: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how epikodelabs handles personal information in connection with the actionstack documentation website, source code repository, package distribution, and related communications.

1. Information We Collect ​

We may collect:

  • Contact information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, or the contents of messages you send
  • Public profile and communication data you submit through platforms such as GitHub issues, pull requests, or discussions
  • Technical data that may be collected by hosting or delivery providers, such as IP address, browser type, device information, referrer, timestamps, and basic request logs

The documentation site does not currently require user accounts and does not currently use first-party analytics or advertising trackers.

2. How We Use Information ​

We may use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Provide documentation, releases, and support
  • Maintain project security and reliability
  • Review contributions and community submissions
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce project policies and license terms

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies ​

actionstack does not currently use first-party advertising cookies or first-party analytics cookies on the documentation site. Third-party hosts or platforms may still use their own cookies or logging technologies when you access their services.

4. Sharing of Information ​

We may share information:

  • With service providers or platforms used to host or distribute the project
  • When required by law or valid legal process
  • To protect rights, safety, security, or prevent abuse
  • In connection with a business transfer involving the project or related assets

We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Retention ​

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including recordkeeping, support, security, and legal compliance.

Public contributions or comments you post on third-party platforms may remain visible according to those platforms' controls and policies.

6. Security ​

Reasonable administrative and technical measures may be used to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Rights ​

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain personal information. To make a request, contact the email below.

8. Third-Party Links and Platforms ​

actionstack may link to GitHub, npm, and other third-party services. Their privacy practices are separate from this policy, and you should review their policies directly.

9. Policy Updates ​

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updated versions become effective when published with a new effective date.

10. Contact ​

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to oleksii.shepel@outlook.com.
