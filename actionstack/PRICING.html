Pricing ​

Effective date: May 7, 2026

actionstack is open-source software. The source code, package, and documentation are available at no charge under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0-only).

Open Source Use ​

Price: $0

This includes:

  • Access to the public source code and documentation
  • Use, modification, and redistribution rights under the AGPL
  • Community support through public channels such as GitHub issues and discussions

This does not include:

  • Service-level agreements
  • Guaranteed response times
  • Private support channels
  • Custom implementation, migration, or feature work

Commercial License ​

For proprietary use without AGPL obligations, a commercial license is available:

  • actionstack: $10 — one-time payment
  • streamix: $25 individual / $30 team — one-time payment

A commercial license removes the AGPL obligation for your own proprietary code and is ideal for SaaS or internal tools. After purchase, your GitHub account is added to a private licensed repository with full source code, tests, examples, and build configuration.

To purchase, sign in with GitHub on epikodelabs.github.io.

Paid Services ​

Optional paid services may be offered by epikodelabs on a case-by-case basis, including:

  • Architecture and implementation consulting
  • Migration support
  • Priority debugging sessions
  • Training or code reviews
  • Custom development work

Pricing for paid services is provided only by separate written quote, proposal, or statement of work.

Commercial License FAQ ​

Are these prices too small?
We intentionally keep one-time licenses affordable for indie developers and small teams — $25/$30 for streamix and $10 for actionstack is competitive for perpetual rights. It helps sustain open-source development while keeping it accessible. For enterprises with custom needs, contact us for customized agreements.

What do I get?
After purchase, your GitHub account will be added to a private licensed repository to gain access to the full source code, tests, examples, and build configuration. You also receive commercial usage rights for proprietary projects without AGPL obligations for your own code.

Payment methods:
PayPal / wire / invoice via email.

Important Notes ​

  • Paid services do not, by themselves, change the AGPL license that applies to this repository unless a separate written agreement explicitly states otherwise.
  • Commercial licenses are delivered digitally and grant perpetual rights.
  • Taxes, payment processor fees, and approved third-party expenses may be added where applicable.
  • Delivery dates, scope, and acceptance criteria for paid services are defined in the applicable written agreement.
